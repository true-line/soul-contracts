# Requirements

Product requirements moved to the platform: [product requirements at baseline/3](https://github.com/true-line/agent-bot-platform/blob/f1008c8cd5e4e1b27125380731f4422b1f392392/docs/02-requirements/product-requirements.md), generated from its `registry/requirements.yaml`.

`platform.lock.json` lists the requirement IDs that exist at the pinned commit, and `scripts/validate.py` fails on a contract artifact that cites any other.

The platform repository is private until it is released; agents and reviewers with access can follow these links.
